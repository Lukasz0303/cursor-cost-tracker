      Critical alert
      
          
          
      
            A modal dialog when the newest query reaches the token or dollar
            threshold. Each query is shown once. Independent of the status-bar
            ! warning.
          
      
          
          
      
          
          
      
            Defaults are 10,000k tokens (10M) or $5. Use − / + to step (100k or
            $0.50), or type the full number. Values save only when you click
            Apply or press Enter — not while stepping or typing.
          
      
          
        
      
        
      
          
      Monthly budget
      
          
          
      
            How daily budget, Today, and the monthly forecast spread the
            remaining cycle. Working days is the default (~22 in September).
            Calendar days uses every day in the month (30 in September).

            depth. After Start, savings go to
            .ai/optimize-savings.md.
